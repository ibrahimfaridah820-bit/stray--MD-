Hey stranger, it's me again.
A stray whisper found its way to me today,
carried on a breeze I never asked to breathe.
It spoke of you, of shadows caught in corners 
where I thought only light lived.
A jagged piece of news,
I try to smooth the surface over with patience.
How strange it is to care for someone's shadow
even when it shifts into a shape you didn't want to see.
Yet even so, my anchor holds its ground in the storm,
refusing to let a sudden squall untie what my heart has bound.

My hunny bunny, you are like a soft ball of fur,
steadily giving a soft landing.
And for that, I will give you grace,
because you are only human,
and with time, I hope you open up before it's too late
